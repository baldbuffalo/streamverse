Made for the living room

Designed around D-pad navigation, big-screen layouts, and the way you actually watch TV.

📺

TV-first UI

Built on androidx.tv Compose components with focus-aware cards and immersive hero banners.

🔐

Google Sign-In

One-tap sign-in through Play Services, bridged to Firebase Auth for a stable per-user identity.

☁️

Synced My List

Your watchlist and history are stored in Firestore and follow your Google account across devices.

🎬

Rich show details

Seasons, episodes, match scores, ratings and tags — a full browsing experience.

⚡

Fast image loading

Coil 3 handles posters, banners and thumbnails with smooth, cached loading.

📱

Responsive

Adaptive layouts scale cleanly from phones (minSdk 23) to large TV displays.

Built with

A fully modern, 100% Kotlin Android stack.
